# Media clearances

The clearance record for everything in `public/media`. A photograph or a video
is not cleared to go live until it has an entry here.

This file is deliberately NOT in `public/`. Everything under `public/` is
served to anyone on the internet who asks for the path, and the round two
review of bead hq-4pu0q.2 caught the clearance template sitting there: it asked
for a real person's name, a client contact's name, and the location of a signed
release form, in a file a visitor can fetch. Names of people who agreed to be
photographed, names of client staff who signed something, and the location of a
signed document are not attribution. They are the paperwork, and paperwork does
not get published.

What stays public, in `public/media/CREDITS.md`, is the credit line and the
licence: who made a picture and on what terms the site uses it. Nothing else.

## Two clearances, both required

A recognisable person needs a release. A client needs to approve their
premises, their staff, and anything of theirs visible on a screen, which on
this site is usually their own production data. When a screen shows real
numbers, say so in the entry and name who approved them.

Both are separate and both are required before a file goes live.

## The entry

One per file that ships:

```
## winery/cellar.webp

- Shot by: Cardon Digital, 2026-10-04
- Location: <winery>, Valle de Guadalupe
- People in frame: <who>
- Release: written permission held, filed at <where>
- Client clearance: premises and on screen data approved by <name>, <date>
- Real numbers on screen: <yes or no; if yes, who approved them>
- Slot: winery/cellar
```
